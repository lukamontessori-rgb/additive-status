Privacy policy

The short version: the site itself collects no personal data. There are no accounts, no cookies, no analytics, no ads, no emails and no third-party trackers. The only personal data involved is what the web host needs to deliver pages, and anything you choose to write in a report.

Who is responsible

The controller for the little personal data described here is the operator of {{ SITE_NAME }}, a private individual{% if OPERATOR.country %} established in {{ OPERATOR.country }}{% endif %}{% if OPERATOR.operator_name %} ({{ OPERATOR.operator_name }}){% endif %}. {{ contact("Contact") }}{% if OPERATOR.postal_address %} Address: {{ OPERATOR.postal_address }}.{% endif %} The operator is not required to have a data protection officer and has not appointed one.

What we collect

Nothing that identifies you. {{ SITE_NAME }} is a set of static pages. It has:

  • No accounts and no sign-up of any kind.
  • No forms that send data. The search box and the label checker work in your browser.
  • No cookies, no analytics, no advertising, no fingerprinting and no session recording.
  • No third-party requests. Every script, style, font and icon is served from this site. There are no embeds, pixels, social buttons or software development kits (SDKs) from other companies.
  • No emails. We never send email, run a newsletter or keep a mailing list, so there is nothing to unsubscribe from.
  • No selling or sharing of data, because there is no data to sell or share. There is no profiling and no automated decision-making about you.
  • No "Do Not Track" or opt-out needed. Because nothing tracks you, there is nothing to opt out of; the site behaves the same whatever signal your browser sends.

Settings kept on your device, with your permission

Nothing is stored and nothing pops up when you arrive. If you choose Remember my settings (under Privacy choices in the footer, or on the cookies page), your browser keeps up to four small settings in its local storage: your light or dark theme, whether you paused the animations, your best quiz score and the country you picked in the label checker. They stay on your device and are never sent to us or anyone else. If you choose Don't remember, only that choice is kept; a theme or animation setting you change then lasts only until you close the tab (session storage). You can withdraw consent at any time: the cookies and storage page lists every item and lets you delete them in one click.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. (a Microsoft company). Like any web host, GitHub processes technical data such as IP addresses to deliver pages and keep the service secure, and GitHub decides for itself how it does so. GitHub handles this data under its own General Privacy Statement. The site operator does not receive these logs or any visitor statistics and cannot identify visitors from them. The site has no cookie banner because nothing here sets cookies.

Search and tools

  • Search. Suggestions are worked out in your browser from a file on this site. If you press Enter, your search words become part of the page address (for example /additives/?q=e171), and the host may record that address like any other page request.
  • Label checker. The ingredients you paste are checked in your browser and never leave your device.
  • Quiz. Your answers stay in your browser. Your best score is kept only if you allowed it.

Reports and messages

Corrections and notices can be sent through GitHub issues, which are public and covered by GitHub's terms and privacy statement{% if OPERATOR.contact_email %}, or by email{% endif %}. Please do not put personal information in a public issue. You can edit or delete what you posted through your GitHub account, or ask the operator to remove it.

Transfers outside your country

GitHub, Inc. is a company in the United States and may process data in the United States and other countries. The safeguards it uses for such transfers are described in its privacy statement. The operator itself does not send personal data anywhere.

Children

The site is suitable for all ages, but it is not aimed at children and does not ask for anyone's age. Because it collects no personal data from anyone, it collects none from children, and no parental consent is needed to use it. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 or under the age of digital consent in your country.

Your rights

Under the GDPR (and the UK GDPR) you have the right to:

  • get access to personal data held about you, and a copy of it,
  • have inaccurate data corrected and have data erased,
  • restrict how data is used, and object to processing based on legitimate interests,
  • receive data you gave us in a common format (data portability),
  • withdraw consent at any time, without affecting what was done before.

The operator holds no personal data about visitors, so there is normally nothing to return, correct or delete, apart from what you sent in a report or message. Settings on your own device can be deleted on the cookies and storage page or in your browser. For data processed by the host, see GitHub's privacy statement, which explains how to make a request to GitHub. {{ contact("To use any of these rights") }} Requests are answered within one month.

Residents of US states with privacy laws (such as California): the site does not sell or share personal information, does not use it for advertising, and does not collect sensitive personal information.
