Accessibility statement

{{ SITE_NAME }} aims to be usable by everyone, and to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA.

Last reviewed {{ LEGAL_UPDATED }}.

What we do

  • Keyboard. Everything works with a keyboard. A "Skip to content" link comes first, focus is always visible, and / jumps to the search box. The quiz can be played with 1–5 and Enter.
  • Never colour alone. Every status has an icon with its own shape and a text label as well as a colour. On very narrow screens, tables show the icon only and keep the label for screen readers.
  • Contrast. Text and controls are checked for contrast in both the light and the dark theme.
  • Charts have tables. Each chart has a table view, and every number in a chart can also be read as text.
  • Motion. The pages move a lot when you scroll, but every animation is decoration: the same text and numbers are always there without it. Press Pause animations in the menu to stop all of it, or set your device to reduce motion and it never starts. The moving band and the animated example on the home page also have their own pause buttons. Nothing flashes.
  • Screen readers. Pages use headings, landmarks, labelled controls and live regions for results. Decorative graphics are hidden from assistive technology.
  • Images. The site uses no photos; its icons are decorative and hidden from screen readers, and every icon button has a text label.
  • Zoom and small screens. Pages reflow down to 320 pixels wide and work at 200% zoom without scrolling sideways, except wide data tables, which scroll inside their own box.
  • Theme. The site follows your light or dark setting, and you can switch it.

How it is tested

Before this version was published, the main pages were checked with automated tools (axe-core and Lighthouse) in both themes, tested by keyboard, and viewed at phone, tablet and desktop widths. Automated checks cannot find every problem, so reports are welcome.

Known limitations

  • The large table of all additives is long. Use the filters at the top, or the search box, to narrow it down.
  • Some official source documents we link to (for example PDFs from government websites) may not be fully accessible. We cannot change them.
