Disclaimer

Not legal advice. Statuses are automated, simplified summaries of official lists. They are not legal, regulatory, medical or nutritional advice. Read the official source linked on each page before you rely on a status.

Not a safety verdict. Whether a substance is on a list is a legal fact about one place. It does not say whether the substance is safe or harmful.
