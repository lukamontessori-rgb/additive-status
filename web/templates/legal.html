The site's code and the text written for it were produced with the help of an AI assistant (Claude, made by Anthropic) under the direction of the operator, who reviews the site and is responsible for it. The statuses themselves are not written by AI: they are produced by tested, automated code from the official sources named above, and every status links to its source. AI systems can make mistakes, so please report anything that looks wrong.

Honest content

  • No reviews or testimonials. The site publishes no reviews, ratings, endorsements or testimonials, real or invented.
