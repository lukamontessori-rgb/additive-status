Disclaimer

Not legal advice. Statuses are automated, simplified summaries of official lists. They are not legal, regulatory, medical or nutritional advice. Before you make a decision that depends on the law — such as making, labelling, importing or selling food — read the official source linked on each page and, where needed, ask the competent authority or a qualified professional.

Not a safety verdict. Whether a substance is on a list is a legal fact about one jurisdiction. It does not say whether the substance is safe or harmful.

Privacy

  • No cookies, no analytics, no advertising, no fingerprinting, no accounts, no newsletter.
  • No third-party requests. Every script, style, font and icon is served from this site. No embeds, pixels or social widgets.
  • The label checker runs in your browser. The ingredients you paste are never sent anywhere.
  • A few settings stay on your device. Your theme choice (light or dark), your best quiz score and the country you chose in the label checker are kept in your browser's local storage so they are there next time. They are never sent to us or anyone else. Clearing your browser's site data removes them.
