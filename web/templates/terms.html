The service

The site shows the status of food additives on the official lists of the European Union, Great Britain, the United States (federal), Canada and Australia/New Zealand, with links to those lists. You do not need an account, and you do not have to give any personal information.

Fees, payments and refunds

Everything on the site is free. There are no fees, purchases, subscriptions, paid features, requests for donations or hidden charges, so there is nothing to pay and nothing to refund.

Not legal, medical or nutritional advice

Statuses are automated, simplified summaries. They can be out of date between weekly checks, and automated reading and matching can go wrong. Only the official publications are authoritative; for EU law, only the texts in the Official Journal of the European Union are authentic. Before you make a decision that depends on the law — such as making, labelling, importing or selling food — read the official source linked on each page and, where needed, ask the competent authority or a qualified professional. Whether a substance is on a list is a legal fact about one place. It is not a judgement about whether the substance is safe.
