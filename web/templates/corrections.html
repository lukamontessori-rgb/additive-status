Report a mistake

{{ contact("Contact") }} Please include:

  • the page address (URL) and the place (EU, GB, US, Canada or Australia/New Zealand) you think is wrong,
  • what you think the correct status is, and a link to the official source that shows it.

Reports on GitHub are public and covered by GitHub's terms and privacy statement, so please do not include personal information in them.

Legal or rights-holder notice

If you believe content on the site infringes copyright, a trade mark or another right, is inaccurate in a way that harms you, or is unlawful, please send a notice that contains:

  1. your name and a way to contact you (an email address is enough),
  2. the exact address (URL) of the page, and a description of the content in question,
  3. an explanation of why you believe it is unlawful or incorrect, and the right you claim (for example, which work you own),
  4. a statement that you are acting in good faith and that the information in your notice is accurate and complete.

{{ contact("Send it to") }} {% if not OPERATOR.contact_email %}Because GitHub issues are public, do not include sensitive personal details in them; say only that you have a notice and how you can be reached.{% endif %}

The site has no user-generated content: there are no comments, uploads or accounts, so the only content is what the operator publishes.

What happens next

  • The operator is one private person, so replies are not instant. Reports are read and answered as soon as practical, and accessibility problems and clear errors come first.
  • If a notice is well founded, the content is corrected or removed quickly, and the change is recorded in the public history of the site's code.
  • If a report is not accepted, you will be told why where you gave a way to reach you.
  • Notices that are plainly unfounded, abusive or repeated may be declined.

Authorities and courts

Requests from authorities and courts are welcome through the same contact. You keep every right you have under the law to go to a court or to the competent authority; for personal data, see the privacy policy.
